# money: 14 findings
I found 14 defects, each reproduced with a scratch probe or traced through the code: 2 critical, 2 high, 6 medium, 4 low. The core capture logic holds up. Each tab gets one final capture with its own fixed PayPal-Request-Id, and only creator-approved cards whose evidence span passed the guard (or was pointed by the creator) are charged. Late backers are not charged. Refunds are capped by refundShare and run one at a time per tab, and webhooks are verified on the raw body and deduplicated. ## F1 [critical/server] A payout can be sent twice after a lost or 5xx response
- file: src/server/domain/payouts.ts:168
- scenario: PayPal accepts the batch but the response times out twice, or PayPal answers 5xx after creating it. The console shows an error, and the creator clicks 'Close contest window' again (or the close_contest_window job retries after its backoff). A second batch with a new sender_batch_id pays the creator the full amount again.
- fix: Mark FAILED only on a definitive refusal: a 4xx that is not isDuplicatePayoutBatch. For httpStatus 0 or >= 500, leave batch_status as 'CREATING'. In closeContestWindow, when the latest row has no payout_batch_id and is not definitively failed, resend with the same attempt (existing.attempt). PayPal then answers with the duplicate-batch link and createPayout recovers the original batch.
- verified: True

## F2 [critical/server] 'Verify with PayPal' after a contest refund flips the tab to Settling and drops its capture from the payout
- file: src/server/domain/receipts.ts:113
- scenario: On the judge path, Chen contests Q2 and the desk refunds $3. Chen or the judge then clicks 'Verify with PayPal' on the receipt to see the refund. The receipt chip changes from Captured to a Settling spinner. The payout then leaves out Chen's whole capture net while still subtracting the $3 refund, so the creator is underpaid and the payout panel shows the wrong amount.
- fix: Treat PARTIALLY_REFUNDED and REFUNDED as completed captures. In recordCapture, use completed = ['COMPLETED','PARTIALLY_REFUNDED','REFUNDED'].includes(cap.status). In payoutInputs, accept the same set instead of status === 'COMPLETED'. At minimum, only call recordCapture from verifyReceipt when tab.state === 'settling'.
- verified: True

## F3 [high/server] Room expiry counts from creation and deletes captured tabs and contests before the payout
- file: src/server/domain/rooms.ts:79
- scenario: A creator sets up a public room early in the week and streams on day 6. Fans are charged at settlement, but expire_room fires at day 7, inside the contest window (or the 24 h approve window), and deletes the tabs. The payout job then computes 0 and the creator is never paid. Receipts and contest history are deleted too.
- fix: Reschedule expire_room from endRoom, finishRoom and the payout's terminal state to max(expires_at, contest_window_ends_at + 7 days). Make expireRoom reschedule itself unless the room is voided, or settled with a terminal payout_state.
- verified: True

## F4 [high/server] Creator and fan tokens are logged in plain text through the k= / t= SSE aliases
- file: src/server/app.ts:32
- scenario: Every console load and SSE reconnect writes the room's creator token to the logs, which on Render run at LOG_LEVEL=info. Anyone with log access, or a pasted log excerpt, can settle rooms, approve contest refunds and trigger payouts for any room, including judges' demo rooms. They can also act as any fan whose page or stream was loaded. The code comment and the test in domain.test.ts claim logged URLs never carry tokens.
- fix: Redact all three names: url.replace(/([?&](?:token|k|t)=)[^&]+/g, '$1REDACTED'). Also drop the unused k/t aliases from api.eventsUrl, since the server reads only token. Add a redaction test that uses the real client URL shape.
- verified: True

## F5 [medium/server] Transient capture errors or a restart leave the room stuck in 'settling' with no retry in the UI
- file: src/server/domain/settle.ts:244
- scenario: A sandbox 5xx, or a deploy during Settle, leaves the console showing 'Settling with PayPal…' with no button. This lasts 30 minutes in demo rooms and 24 hours in public rooms, until auto_void resumes it. Receipts, the contest window and the payout are blocked meanwhile, even though the server message says the tab will be reconciled on the next settle.
- fix: Show a 'Retry settlement' button when status is 'settling' and no settle is in flight; POST /settle already resumes. Also schedule a short retry job (about 1 min) when doSettle returns unknown-outcome errors, and resume 'settling' rooms at boot.
- verified: True

## F6 [medium/server] A contest left in 'deciding' (desk lost in a restart) can never be decided and blocks the payout forever
- file: src/server/domain/contests.ts:265
- scenario: The server restarts while Opus is working a contest (a desk run takes 1-3 minutes). The contest stays 'deciding' forever. The creator's Approve/Keep buttons get 409, the demo control says to decide open contests first, and the payout job rechecks every minute indefinitely. The creator is never paid.
- fix: At boot, move contests left in 'open' or 'deciding' to 'failed' so the creator decides them, or re-run the desk. Let decideContest accept a 'deciding' contest older than a few minutes.
- verified: True

## F7 [medium/server] A refused capture marks the tab failed but never voids the hold, and the room then shows as voided
- file: src/server/domain/settle.ts:246
- scenario: PayPal refuses a capture for a business reason such as instrument declined, payee restricted or duplicate invoice. The console reports the room as voided and the hold as released, but the fan's full hold stays on their card until PayPal expires it (up to 29 days).
- fix: In the definitive-refusal branch, GET the authorization first. If it is still CREATED, void it before markFailed (voidTab, next attempt suffix) and set released_cents accordingly.
- verified: True

## F8 [medium/server] Rooms that are never ended keep every hold, and expire_room deletes the tabs without voiding
- file: src/server/domain/lifecycle.ts:11
- scenario: A judge leaves a demo room after the autoplay without clicking End, which is likely. The three bot holds and the judge's own card-fields hold are never released by AnswerTab. After 7 days the authorization IDs are deleted from the DB, so they can no longer be voided. The same happens to real fans in abandoned public rooms.
- fix: Schedule an auto-end or auto-void job when a room goes live (for example, start + 12 h for demo rooms and + 48 h for public rooms). Make expireRoom void any tab that has a live authorization before deleting rows.
- verified: True

## F9 [medium/ai] Concurrent contest desks blow through the per-room and per-pool LLM caps
- file: src/server/ai/budget.ts:71
- scenario: A public user creates a room, adds sandbox test-card fans, settles, and fires every contest at once. Each concurrent desk passes the cap check, so the room and the $2/day public pool are overspent several times per burst. Repeating across rooms costs real Anthropic money.
- fix: Reserve before the call with a conditional UPDATE: rooms.llm_spent_micros = llm_spent_micros + est WHERE llm_spent_micros + est <= cap RETURNING, and the same for llm_budget. Correct it by the real cost afterwards. Also queue contest desks one at a time per room, and estimate Opus turns from max_tokens.
- verified: True

## F10 [medium/paypal] Forged webhooks trigger real PayPal verify calls through the shared 4-slot limiter, and their full payloads are stored
- file: src/server/domain/webhooks.ts:52
- scenario: An attacker posts forged events at 240/min from a few IPs. Verify calls occupy the 4 PayPal slots, so bot holds, captures and voids queue behind them during a judge's demo. Meanwhile webhook_events grows by about 30 MB per minute per IP.
- fix: Before calling PayPal, require paypal-cert-url to be https on api.paypal.com or api.sandbox.paypal.com and paypal-transmission-time to be recent. Store only id and event_type for unverified events. Give webhook verification its own small limiter so it cannot starve settlement.
- verified: True

## F11 [low/paypal] Genuine webhooks are lost when the verify API fails transiently
- file: src/server/paypal/webhooks.ts:82
- scenario: A genuine CUSTOMER.DISPUTE.CREATED or PAYMENT.PAYOUTSBATCH.SUCCESS arrives while the verify call times out. It is never processed: hasOpenDispute stays false, so contest refunds are not blocked during the dispute, and the payout webhook badge never appears.
- fix: Have verifyWebhook return a separate 'unknown' result when the call fails without a verification_status. Answer 503 from the route in that case so PayPal retries delivery.
- verified: True

## F12 [low/server] One transient error after the 5th payout poll stops payout polling for good
- file: src/server/jobs/sweeper.ts:59

- fix: Count polls separately, for example in payload.polls, and stop counting them in attempts. Alternatively, exempt poll_payout from MAX_ATTEMPTS while polls < MAX_POLLS.
- verified: True

## F13 [low/server] A capture still PENDING at payout time is never paid to the creator
- file: src/server/domain/payouts.ts:71
- scenario: A wallet capture is PENDING (for example PENDING_REVIEW) when the contest window closes. The fan is charged once it completes, but that money is never paid out to the creator.
- fix: While any capture in the room is PENDING, return { again } from closeContestWindow instead of paying. Alternatively, send a top-up batch (next attempt) for captures that complete after the payout.
- verified: True

## F14 [low/server] Anyone with a room link can use up the room's 500 SSE slots and lock out the creator's console
- file: src/server/sse.ts:74

## F1 [critical/server] Verify with PayPal after a contest refund flips the tab back to 'settling'; the payout then drops that capture but still subtracts its refund
- file: src/server/domain/receipts.ts:113
- scenario: Judge contests Q2 on a receipt, the desk refunds $3, and the judge clicks 'Verify with PayPal' again to see the refund. That tab is stuck at 'Settling' everywhere. 'Close contest window' then underpays the creator by that fan's whole capture net ($5.35 here). A full refund (status REFUNDED) has the same effect.
- fix: Treat PARTIALLY_REFUNDED and REFUNDED as settled captures. In recordCapture, completed = ['COMPLETED','PARTIALLY_REFUNDED','REFUNDED'].includes(status), giving state 'captured' and auth CAPTURED. In payoutInputs, accept those statuses too (refunds are already subtracted separately). In verifyReceipt, call recordCapture only when the stored status is PENDING.
- verified: True

## F2 [critical/web] Review list is never refreshed after End: a card that locks after End shows as 'not answered — released', yet 'Settle (void every hold)' charges it
- file: src/web/pages/console/useConsole.ts:68
- scenario: The creator or judge types an answer, or watches the autoplay's 'verifying' moment, and clicks End while Haiku or Opus is still working (1-5 s). Server: stop applying detector and verifier results after End (or send them to 'review'), and charge a locked card only when the settle body carries an explicit rating for it (409 otherwise).
